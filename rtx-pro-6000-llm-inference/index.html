One per GPU: 128 and 307 tok/s, no interference.

  • Tensor parallel, not pipeline: TP2 beats PP2 by ~40% even without NVLink; expert parallel changes nothing.
  • Speculative decoding is the biggest lever for one user, with no accuracy change.
  • Eval settings move scores ~20 points. Use each vendor's sampling and 100K+ tokens, never a small cap.
  • 1017 is huge (763B params): it only fits as a 2-bit GGUF in llama.cpp (138 GB on GPU, 110 GB Engram on SSD).

Caveats: 40 problems, one sample each (about ±12–15 points per model; only DeepSeek vs Inkling and vs Qwen3.6-35B is statistically clear). Contest problems likely predate training data.
